# Integrate the conversation into shopper ticket flows

Status: completed

Replace the shopper’s fragmented ticket-detail cards with the shared conversation experience in both the ticket-list drawer and direct ticket route. Preserve existing order links, replacement-order actions, unread behavior, and deep links.

## Acceptance Criteria

- “查看工单” opens the focused right-side conversation drawer without leaving the list.
- Direct ticket links render the same messages and business cards.
- Evidence upload, ordinary reply, close, and reply-to-reopen refresh detail, list, order, and unread state as applicable.
- Terminal applications retain their replacement-order action and cannot be presented as payable.
- The interaction is usable by keyboard and at mobile widths.
- Existing user-visible strings are localized.
- Page-level tests cover existing, new, closed, and terminal tickets.
