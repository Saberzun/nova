# Integrate the conversation into administrator review

Status: completed

Embed the shared conversation in the existing wider administrator review drawer while preserving Receipt Verification, approval, rejection, request-more-information, receipt, and refund controls.

Administrator public replies and internal notes must remain distinguishable. Ticket actions must remain operationally separate from Receipt Verification and order settlement.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The administrator sees the same chronological messages and business cards as the shopper, adjusted for administrator perspective.
- Public replies and internal notes are visually and semantically distinct.
- Payment Evidence original images are available only through authorized reads.
- Existing Receipt Verification and settlement controls remain outside the ordinary composer.
- Ticket close/reopen controls cannot invoke financial actions.
- Review actions refresh the conversation and authoritative review data.
- Administrator interaction tests cover public reply, internal note, evidence inspection, close/reopen, and financial-state isolation.
